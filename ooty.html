Welcome to Ooty

Explore the cultural and historical richness of the Athens of the East

Ooty Lake

An artificial lake constructed for fishing purposes, Ooty Lake is now a popular spot for boating. Visitors can enjoy serene boat rides amidst picturesque surroundings.

Government Botanical Garden

Established in 1842, this garden boasts a vast collection of indigenous and exotic plants. A highlight is the annual flower show held in May, attracting numerous visitors.

Government Rose Garden

Situated on the slopes of Elk Hill, this garden is home to over 20,000 varieties of roses, making it the largest rose garden in India. It’s a visual treat for nature enthusiasts.

Doddabetta Peak

Standing as one of the highest peaks in Tamil Nadu, Doddabetta offers panoramic views of the Nilgiri Mountains. On clear days, visitors can even spot the city of Coimbatore.

Nilgiri Mountain Railway

(Toy Train): A UNESCO World Heritage site, this train journey provides scenic vistas of forests, tunnels, and mist-covered landscapes, capturing the essence of Ooty’s charm.

Pykara Lake and Waterfalls

Located amidst dense forests, Pykara Lake is a serene spot ideal for boating. The nearby Pykara Waterfalls add to the area’s natural beauty, making it a favored picnic destination.

Avalanche Lake

Nestled amidst a captivating landscape filled with blooming flowers, Avalanche Lake is a haven for nature enthusiasts and photographers. The surrounding waterfalls further enhance its allure.

Tea Factory and Museum

This establishment offers insights into the tea-making process, showcasing the machinery used and the history of tea production in the region. Visitors can also sample and purchase local teas.
