Welcome to Bangalore

Explore the cultural and historical richness of the Athens of the East

Bangalore Palace

A stunning palace inspired by England’s Windsor Castle, featuring beautiful interiors and gardens.

Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace

A historic palace showcasing Indo-Islamic architecture and the legacy of Tipu Sultan.

ISKCON Temple

– A grand temple dedicated to Lord Krishna, offering spiritual and architectural beauty.

Bull Temple (Nandi Temple)

A famous temple with a huge monolithic statue of Nandi (the sacred bull).

St. Mary’s Basilica

One of the oldest churches in Bangalore, known for its Gothic-style architecture.

Lalbagh Botanical Garden

A 240-acre garden featuring a variety of exotic plants and a beautiful glass house.

Cubbon Park

A lush green park in the heart of the city, ideal for walking and relaxation.

Visvesvaraya Museum

Visvesvaraya Industrial & Technological Museum – A great place for science enthusiasts, with interactive exhibits.

National Gallery of Modern Art

(NGMA) Showcases Indian modern art with a vast collection of paintings and sculptures.

Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium

Offers amazing space and astronomy shows.

Wonderla Amusement Park

A thrilling amusement park with water rides and roller coasters.

Innovative Film City

A theme park offering movie sets, adventure sports, and fun activities.

MG Road & Brigade Road

Popular shopping and nightlife destinations in Bangalore.

Commercial Street

A great place for street shopping and trendy fashion.

UB City Mall

A luxury shopping and dining hub.

Nandi Hills (60 km from Bangalore)

A scenic hill station offering breathtaking sunrise views.

Savandurga

Savandurga (50 km) – A large monolithic hill ideal for trekking.

Bannerghatta National Park

A wildlife reserve with a zoo and safari experiences.
