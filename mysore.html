Welcome to Mysore

Explore the cultural and historical richness of the Athens of the East

Mysore Palace

• One of the most famous landmarks in India • Beautiful Indo-Saracenic architecture • Illuminated with thousands of lights every Sunday and during Dasara

Chamundi Hill & Chamundeshwari Temple

• A sacred hill with a famous temple dedicated to Goddess Chamundeshwari • Offers a panoramic view of Mysore city • Nandi statue, a huge monolithic bull, is a major attraction

St. Philomena’s Church

• One of the tallest churches in Asia • Built in Neo-Gothic style with stunning stained glass windows

Mysore Zoo

(Sri Chamarajendra Zoological Gardens) • One of the oldest and best-maintained zoos in India • Home to a variety of animals, including tigers, elephants, and giraffes

Karanji Lake

• A peaceful lake with a beautiful nature park • Houses a large butterfly park and an aviary

Jaganmohan Palace & Art Gallery

• Houses one of the largest collections of paintings in South India • Displays artworks by Raja Ravi Varma and other famous artists

Railway Museum

• Showcases the history of Indian Railways • Features old locomotives and royal train coaches

Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary

(Nearby, ~20 km) • A paradise for bird watchers • Home to migratory birds like pelicans, storks, and herons

Srirangapatna

Srirangapatna (Nearby, ~15 km) • Historical town associated with Tipu Sultan • Attractions include Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace, Ranganathaswamy Temple, and Gumbaz

Lalitha Mahal Palace

• A luxurious heritage hotel with royal architecture • Offers great views of Mysore from the hilltop

Sand Museum

• Unique museum showcasing amazing sand sculptures • Features themes like Mysore heritage, wildlife, and famous personalities

Melody World Wax Museum

• A musical-themed wax museum • Displays life-size wax statues of musicians and musical instruments
