A beautiful beachfront perfect for walking and enjoying the sunrise.

Known for its pristine beauty, accessible via a boat ride.

Auroville Beach

Ideal for swimming and relaxation.

Serenity Beach

A quiet beach, perfect for surfing and sunbathing.

Sri Aurobindo Ashram

A peaceful spiritual retreat founded by Sri Aurobindo.

Auroville

An experimental universal township promoting human unity; don’t miss the Matrimandir, a golden meditation dome.

Manakula Vinayagar Temple

A famous Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Ganesha.

Immaculate Conception Cathedral

A beautiful church built in 1791.

French Quarter (White Town)

Known for its colonial-era buildings, colorful streets, and cafes.

Bharathi Park

A lush green space surrounded by historical buildings.

Old Lighthouse

A historical landmark with stunning views of the sea.

Pondicherry Museum

Showcases artifacts from French colonial history and ancient sculptures.

French War Memorial

A tribute to soldiers who died during World War.

Chunnambar Boat House

A boating and picnic spot near Paradise Beach.

Botanical Garden

A peaceful place with exotic plants and trees.

Shopping at Goubert Market

Explore local produce, crafts, and spices.

Cafes and Restaurants

Relish French cuisine and seafood in quaint cafes like Café des Arts or Le Dupleix.
