Welcome to Madurai

Explore the cultural and historical richness of the Athens of the East

Meenakshi Amman Temple

A majestic temple dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi, featuring 14 towering gopurams and over 30,000 sculptures.

Thirumalai Nayak Palace

A blend of Dravidian and Rajput architecture, famous for its grand pillars and light and sound show. including additional details.

Gandhi Memorial Museum

A tribute to Mahatma Gandhi, featuring relics from India’s freedom struggle. including his blood-stained garment.

Koodal Azhagar Temple

A stunning temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, known for its Navagraha idols, a rarity in Vishnu temples.

Alagar Kovil

A scenic temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, famous for the Chithirai Festival and breathtaking hilltop views.

Thiruparankundram Murugan Temple

One of the six abodes of Lord Murugan, carved out of a hill, attracting devotees from all over India.

Pazhamudircholai

A peaceful temple of Lord Murugan, nestled in dense forests, offering a spiritual and natural retreat.
