Welcome to Mahabalipuram

Explore the cultural and historical richness of the Athens of the East

Shore Temple

A stunning Dravidian-style temple overlooking the Bay of Bengal.

Pancha Rathas

(Five Rathas) – Monolithic rock-cut temples dedicated to the Pandavas.

Arjuna’s Penance

A massive rock relief depicting scenes from Hindu mythology.

Krishna’s Butter Ball

A giant balancing boulder that defies gravity.

Varaha Cave Temple

A rock-cut cave temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu.

Mahishasuramardini Cave Temple

Features intricate carvings of Goddess Durga slaying Mahishasura.

Trimurti Cave Temple

Dedicated to the Hindu trinity of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva.

Mahabalipuram Beach

A serene beach popular for relaxation and photography.

Crocodile Bank

A reptile conservation center housing various species of crocodiles and alligators.

Sculpture Museum

Showcases ancient sculptures and artifacts.

India Seashell Museum

Displays a vast collection of rare seashells.
